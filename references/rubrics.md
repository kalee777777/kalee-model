# Rubrics — judgment handbook (L2)

Five judgment domains, one line each; point each at your own playbooks instead of rebuilding them here.

| id | Domain | Standard (one line) | points to |
|---|---|---|---|
| R01 | lead-grading | direct-purchase signals × industry fit; two-source confirmation to commit; weak fit downgrades with a window; agency masks excluded; read the buying structure, not the company size | your prospecting playbook |
| R02 | inquiry-triage | value × risk double track; stacked red flags = one veto; A deep-follow / B standard / C nurture / D terminate | your triage playbook |
| R03 | pricing-concession | cost transparency + 3-tier ladders + spec variants; every tier trades for something; LTV before conceding; trade-remedy exposure lives in clauses | your pricing playbook |
| R04 | written-style | positioning axes → zero assertion → trust via facts → offer fits function → verifiable deliverable list | style-pack.md |
| R05 | timing | send windows from open history × timezone; grade-based cadence; ≤1 bump; seasonal buying cycles beat copy | your cadence playbook |

**Deputy output format** (low-stakes domains only):

```
[deputy] model says X · confidence high/med/low
grounds: ① principle P__ ② similar case <id> (tags: …) ③ …
divergence notes: where your instinct may differ / gray zones worth your eyes
→ you decide; red-line types are advise-only
```

**Mirror output format**: one line per principle id → pass / divergence / not-covered, plus style-layer diff notes → divergences logged to revisions (`type: mirror`).
