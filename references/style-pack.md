# Style pack — rules + exemplar slots

> Your style layer. Two parts: **rules** (how you sound, distilled from your own best drafts) and **exemplar slots** (pointers to *your* best real drafts, which stay private — this repo ships the slot system, not the exemplars).

## Style rules (a starting set — rewrite from your own diffs)

1. **Zero assertion** — never invent facts about the reader ("I noticed you purchase…") unless publicly verifiable; cite only what they or public records said
2. **Trust rides on verifiable specifics** — delete the meta-talk ("this is not a pitch", "no script, no staging"); a checkable deliverable list carries the trust
3. **Plain and concrete** — facts once, in one line; numbers precise enough to act on; no adjective armor
4. **Offer fits the function** — what you attach/propose matches the recipient's actual job (supply side gets cost benchmarks, quality gets qualification packs, technical gets parameter answers)
5. **Tone bounds** — no aggressive constructions, no pressure framing; follow-ups deliver value (intel / a ready artifact), never "just checking in"
6. **Structure** — short paragraphs, one offer per message, subject lines name the deliverable ("A cost benchmark and a quality file — for your mesh line" style), not adjectives
