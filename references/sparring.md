# Sparring protocol — red/blue negotiation sandbox

> Verb: `spar <counterpart> [standard/savage/tricky]`. The red agent plays the counterpart; the blue side answers *only* by citing a principle + a logged case; you write your own version afterwards — **the diff is the training**. Nothing generated here ever leaves the session, and nothing here gets sent.

## Setup (the agent does this)

1. **Load the counterpart**: your profile/CRM notes on them — decision role, fears, angle, weight; requirements and counters; dos/don'ts from your playbook. No profile? Generate from industry archetype + country playbook and label every assumption 【inferred】.
2. **Load your ammo**: decision-log retrieval on the negotiation theme; constitution + rubrics loaded.
3. **Scenario card** (pick one stall type or invent): silent / price-squeeze / comparison / verification / process / risk — or a custom scene ("three刁钻 questions at the factory-tour call").
4. **Intensity**: standard (a normal buyer) / savage (20% squeeze + competitor anchor) / tricky (audit threats, trade-remedy diggings, old-thread resurrections).

## Rules of engagement (three rounds)

- Red opens each round with one move (email or meeting voice, per the scenario card). Blue must **show its grounds before answering**: one principle id + at least one logged case id.
- Every blue violation is logged: assertion crept in / aggressive phrasing / concession without trade / pressure framing / meta-talk — one point each.
- Red breakthroughs are logged: attacks blue could only fend off with "human judgment" fallbacks — that list *is* your real weak zone.

## Deliverables (in-session)

1. Full transcript (red vs blue, blue's citations inline)
2. Report card: violation list (which principle breaks easiest) / red's three hardest moves / ammo hit-rate
3. **Your version**: you write the reply to the same scenario (or dictate); the agent diffs you vs blue — the diff is the training output, more than winning
4. Capture: new judgments → decision log (`type: other`, tags include `sparring`); constitution gaps → revision proposals; style drift → style-pack slot candidates

## Discipline

- Savage lines never leave the session; real outbound still goes through `mirror` + the red-line list
- 【inferred】 assumptions about the counterpart must be re-verified (evidence tiers) before the real negotiation
- Cadence: one same-scenario spar before an important negotiation (~15 min); ≤2 per week — over-training eats real outreach
